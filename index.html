Music

Hold SWING to shoot a line and let go to fly. Jump twice for a double jump. Hold JET to fly with your jet pack; it refuels when you land. Land on robots to bonk them. Tap a gadget at the top to switch. Pick a song under Music; pause the game to change it.
Keyboard: arrows move · space jumps · Z swings · X fires · C jet pack · 1–4 gadgets

Add to Home Screen

Put WebMaster on the Home Screen so it opens like an app, full screen and without internet.

  1. 1Tap the Share button in Safari. On iPad it is at the top right; on iPhone it is at the bottom. If you can't see it, tap ··· first.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. 3Check the name says WebMaster, then tap Add.
  4. 4Open WebMaster from the new icon once while online. After that it plays offline.
